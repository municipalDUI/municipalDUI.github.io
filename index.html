About

I write personal essays — the kind that begin somewhere ordinary and keep walking until the treeline closes behind them.

Most of the work comes out of time spent outdoors, away from screens. I'm interested in the places where the familiar goes strange: the quiet that isn't empty, the trail that doesn't quite end where the map says.
