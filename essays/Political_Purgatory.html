In many ways I had entirely written off the 2024 election. I wanted to believe, it's easy to fall into the escapism of what could be. I like to dream about where this country could go if we ever took back power, but I know better than to delude myself.

2020 was a revolution, one that I didn't realize until long after. I spent all of 2022 trying to right those wrongs. I really believed we could make things work in Pennsylvania, and from there; go on to save our country, or at least some of it, in 24’. I wanted to stand alongside the men of action who might actually do it. In the end our fight was in vain, the Democrats had secured their election through harvesting mail-in ballots by August, a time when our field team was only beginning to ramp up its canvassing efforts.

These realizations painted a different picture of politics for me. The punches I took completely de-energized, and even “de-radicalized” my political inclinations. I came to accept that I was “along for the ride” for a little while, and it was best to keep my head down - Soviet in retrospect.

I speculated moving into sales, or marketing, something I could escape the impending Covid economic drain with. Those efforts never formulated into anything real. But the least of my expectations would be that 2024 could give us a repeat of the “Summer of Love.”

George Floyd’s death and subsequent mass mobilizations were an absolute shock to me in 2020. My initial reactions were completely sympathetic to Floyd, the videos shared were cropped only to show his frantic final moments before succumbing to his injuries. It was powerful stuff, to hear a man cry out for his life. But as time went on, details of his criminal past emerged, other angles became available, and it became entirely clear that George Floyd had died of a drug overdose, to no fault of Derek Chauvin. By then however, Floyd had emerged as their martyr.

Protests became riots, leaders emboldened them. COVID restrictions remained in play for everyone EXCEPT those who embarked in the name of Floyd. Cities burned, Autonomous zones formed, and shadowed figures threatened their will in the suburbs.

Everything began to swirl. I sat powerless wondering “Who will save us?” Too many stood by, and ultimately Trump saw his political demise. This is a revolution in its purest form, complete mobilization of the masses and a transition of political power.

Reflecting on the left's victory, it solidified my idea that nothing could happen in 2024, “They’ve already got the guy they want in, what’s there to riot over this election cycle?” I’d analyze and re-analyze possibilities in 2023, but no conclusions ever lead to a repeat of Summer 2020.

Man was I wrong. I failed in having any clairvoyance for what was to come. What I mean is the “Bolshevik” types, the actual ANTIFA capable, and willing to use violence, alongside the DSA/College Socialist “Menshevik” types cannibalizing the same Liberals they formed ranks with in 2020. It is our emerging 1917 moment, but somehow my own history evades me.

2020 was a demand for reform, a vision shared by socialists and liberals alike. Go check the homicide charts and ask me if they got their way. By 2023 some, and by some I mean a few, violence trends began to peak and “reverse,” but a generation of criminals was still emboldened.

It all brings me back to early August of 2020 sitting alone on my floor reading Fred Reed's blog post on UNZ titled “It’s all gonna blow.”

“To collapsing countries these usually comes a man on a horse. A man with a big stick. You know, 1789, 1917, 1933. That kind of thing.”

America’s left were rewarded with an eventual change policing procedure. They won’t just “back off” of this one. Like in 1968, the war protests only faded as our role in Vietnam diminished.

What's most interesting is the split in Jews this time around. Those who led on the forefront of dismantling “Racist White Power Structures” in 2020 (and before) have now seen their golems turn on them in supporting decolonization of the West Bank and Gaza. To understand the entire picture, you’ve got to recognize the role that Jews have gladly taken up in America’s social revolutions since the 1960’s. In their (successful) attempt at dismantling WASP hegemony, they failed to divest themselves from the shadow of white identity. In many cases, American Jews have existed in the category of “White with an asterisk”.

And now, the same foreigners they supported refugee status for, the same "anti-racist" education curriculums they helped develop, and the same grassroots organizations they helped to found have all turned on them, even non-religious ethnic Jews show them the cold shoulder, why?

The failure to assimilate and Americanize the bulk of post-1965 immigrants, who after generations of anti-white propaganda, can only see color, and cannot distinguish between “white with an asterisk” Jews and founding stock “huwhite” Gentiles. This is the bulk of your Pro-Palestinian camp. Their struggle is one against “White settler colonialism,” and Zionism to them is just another form of “Whiteness.”

It becomes clear that it was only by chance that Jews got lumped in with us, and this puts Pro-Israel religious Jews in a very precarious situation - Support the destruction of your own people and homeland by allying with those who hate you (NOT happening), or allying with suburban Whites you’ve always blended in with, but remained wary of.

You’ve got to understand that in order for ANY broad “grassroots” push back to come against the Palestinian camp, that it would necessitate the fostering of a national spirit and PRO white sentiments - Something Jews have vehemently opposed because well, look at what happened to them in Germany. Luckily, for them, big players like AIPAC have secured an amass of pro-jewish legislators. One’s who take no restraint in throwing around the force of the state on its political enemies - That is until our next Kent State or George Floyd moment arises.

So what is next? I write this on the morning of May 3rd, 2024 Sometimes you don’t exactly get to choose your teammates, but nonetheless, an enemy is still an enemy, and circumstance is still circumstance - and when it all comes to a head that we stand together in bludgeoning socialists, then I say, “So be it.”
